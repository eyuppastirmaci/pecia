# Team handbook

This handbook collects how we work. Ask in the team channel if something is missing.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the office's time zone. Outside core hours, plan your day as you like. Let the team
know in the channel when you are away for more than half a day.

## Meetings

We keep meetings short and write notes for every decision. Planning happens every second Monday, the retrospective
every second Friday. Anyone may cancel a meeting that has no agenda.

## Code review

Every change needs one approval. Reviewers answer within one working day. Small changes are better than large ones;
split work that touches more than one service.

## On-call

Engineers join the on-call rotation after three months. A shift lasts one week, from Monday 10:00. The handover is
written in the on-call log and discussed in a short call.

### Compensation

Each on-call week is paid as a flat allowance, plus time off for every night someone was paged.

## Expenses

Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days. Purchases above 300 euros need approval from your lead first.

### Travel

Book trains for trips under five hours and economy flights otherwise. Hotels should stay below 150 euros a night in
most cities.

### Conferences

Each person may attend one conference a year at the company's cost, including travel.

## Learning

Every person has a learning budget of 1,000 euros a year for books, courses, and workshops.

## Remote work

Everyone may work remotely up to four days a week. Fully remote contracts are possible after the trial period.

### Home office

The company pays a one-time home office stipend of 750 euros for a desk, chair, and monitor, and a monthly internet
allowance of 30 euros. A company laptop is provided and must be returned when you leave.

### Security at home

Lock your screen when you leave the desk and never let family members use the company laptop.

## Leaving the company

Return your laptop and badge on your last day. Your accounts are closed the same evening.
