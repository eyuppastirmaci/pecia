# Shipping

## Delivery times

Parcels leave the warehouse every weekday before noon. Standard delivery takes two to four working days within the
EU; express delivery arrives the next working day for orders placed before 11:00.

## Costs

Standard delivery costs 4.90 euros. Orders of 40 euros or more ship free of charge. Express delivery always costs 9.90
euros.

## Tracking

Every parcel gets a tracking number when it is dispatched, and the dispatch email links to the courier's tracking page.
