# Privacy

We keep personal data only as long as we need it.

## Retention

Order records are kept for ten years because tax law requires it. Marketing preferences are deleted two years after a
customer's last order. Server logs are kept for 30 days.

## Deletion requests

A customer can ask us to erase their account. Support confirms the request by email, and the account, addresses, and
saved preferences are deleted within 30 days. Order records required for tax purposes are kept but anonymised.

## Access requests

Customers can download their data from the account page as a JSON file.
