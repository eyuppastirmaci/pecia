# Security policy

## Passwords

Customer passwords must be at least 12 characters long. They are hashed with Argon2id. Staff accounts additionally
require a second factor.

## Account recovery

Customers who lose access use the reset form; we email a single-use link that expires after 30 minutes. Support never
asks for a password and never sets one on a customer's behalf.

## Reporting issues

Security reports go to the security mailbox and are answered within two working days.
