# Returns

This page describes how a customer sends a parcel back. Refund amounts and timing are covered by the refund policy.

## Requesting a return

The customer opens the order in their account and chooses "Return items". The shop emails a prepaid label that is
valid for 14 days.

## At the warehouse

Returned parcels are checked in within two working days. Staff inspect the items and mark each line as resellable,
damaged, or missing.

## Exchanges

We do not exchange items directly; the customer returns the item and places a new order.
