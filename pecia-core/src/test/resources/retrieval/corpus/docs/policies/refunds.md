# Refund policy

Customers may ask for a refund within 30 days of delivery.

## How the money comes back

We refund the original payment method. Card refunds reach the customer's account within five business days after
the returned parcel is checked in at the warehouse. Gift card purchases are refunded as store credit immediately.

## Partial refunds

Opened box sets are refunded minus a restocking fee of 15 percent. Damaged or wrong items are always refunded in full,
including the original delivery charge.

## Exceptions

Digital downloads cannot be refunded once the file has been opened.
